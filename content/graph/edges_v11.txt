# v11: links for concepts added from the MIT, Caltech and Stanford course review
pruning > regularization | L1 penalties and magnitude criteria decide which weights go to zero.
pruning > tensorcores | 2:4 structured sparsity runs on sparse tensor cores at up to twice the dense rate.
pruning > distill | A pruned model usually recovers its accuracy by distilling from the dense one.
pruning > quant | Pruning and quantization are combined to shrink models for edge and serving.
llmserving > pruning | Sparse or pruned weights cut memory and compute per token.
embedded > pruning | On-device models are pruned to fit small memory and power budgets.
chainrep > replication | Chain replication is a strongly consistent way to keep copies.
chainrep > failuredetect | A master detects failed chain nodes and splices them out.
chainrep > consmodels | Reads at the tail give linearizable results.
chainrep > tcp | Each link in the chain is a TCP stream of updates.
hdfs > chainrep | An HDFS block write flows through a pipeline of DataNodes, one after another.
dfs > chainrep | Many distributed file systems replicate blocks along a chain or pipeline.
kbypass > dma | The NIC DMAs packets straight into user-space buffers.
kbypass > interrupts | Polling on a dedicated core replaces one interrupt per packet.
kbypass > syscalls | No system call is made on the data path.
kbypass > zerocopy | Packets are never copied between kernel and user space.
kbypass > sockets | It replaces the socket API with its own queues and a user-space TCP stack.
scylla > kbypass | Seastar, ScyllaDB's engine, can run its own TCP stack over DPDK.
redpanda > kbypass | Redpanda is built on Seastar's thread-per-core, kernel-bypass-capable design.
graphemb > embeddings | Each node becomes a dense vector, like a word embedding.
graphemb > markov | Random walks over the graph are Markov chains.
graphemb > matfact | DeepWalk-style training implicitly factorizes a matrix of node co-occurrences.
graphemb > graphtheory | Walks, neighbourhoods and degrees come from graph theory.
graphemb = gnn : alt | Shallow walk-based embeddings versus learned message passing.
recsys > graphemb | Pin and item graphs give embeddings for candidate retrieval.
idgraph > graphemb | Device and account graphs use embeddings to score likely matches.
itp > typesys | Proofs are programs in a dependent type theory (Curry–Howard).
itp > hoare | Program proofs state pre- and postconditions and prove them in the assistant.
itp > logic | Theorems are written in higher-order logic.
itp > proofs | Machine-checked proofs follow the same induction a human proof uses.
formalverif > itp | Large verified systems such as seL4 and CompCert were proved in proof assistants.

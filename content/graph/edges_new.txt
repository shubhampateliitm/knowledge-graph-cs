## New architecture links
commitlog > logcompaction, tieredstorage | A log can keep only the latest value per key, and push old segments to cheap storage.
pubsub > consumergroups | Each group shares the partitions of a topic among its members.
lakehouse > zorder, compaction, schemaevol | Lakehouse performance depends on file layout and size; tables must evolve safely.
mpp > volcano | The classic way query engines connect operators.
microservices > retries, circuitbreaker, outbox | Network calls fail, so services retry carefully, stop calling broken dependencies and publish events reliably.
servicemesh > retries, circuitbreaker, mtls | The mesh applies these policies to every call.
coordination > fencing | Locks from a coordination service are only safe with fencing tokens.
nosql > antientropy | Leaderless replicas need repair mechanisms to converge.
newsql > dst | Distributed databases are tested by deterministic simulation.
blockchain > pbft, pow, merkle | Untrusted participants agree through costly proposals or Byzantine voting, and blocks are Merkle-summarized.
commitlog > shardpercore | Kafka-compatible logs such as Redpanda use one thread per core.
cloudcomp, orchestration, serverless > autoscaling | Capacity follows load automatically.
security, apigateway > oauth | Tokens carry identity and permissions on every request.
security > mtls | Services authenticate each other with certificates.
llm > scalinglaws, cot, decoding | Bigger models trained on more data improve predictably; reasoning and sampling shape the output.
reasoningmodels > cot, rlhf, scalinglaws | Reasoning models are trained with RL to produce long chains of thought.
multimodal > contrastive, attention, embeddings | Images and text are mapped into a shared embedding space.
llmserving > specdecode, promptcache | Drafting tokens and reusing prefixes cut cost and latency.
agents > promptcache | Long agent sessions reuse the same prefix every turn.
cv > contrastive | Vision models learn from image–text pairs.
ml > evals, crossval | You only know a model works by testing it on unseen data.
llm > evals | LLMs are compared on benchmarks and task suites.
ml > experimentation | Models are judged in production with A/B tests.
mediastream > dct, motioncomp, abr | Compression by frequency and motion, delivery by adaptive chunks.
mediastream > perception | Lossy compression throws away what people don't notice.
contentid > fingerprint, perceptualhash | Recognition rests on robust hashes of the signal.
adtech > auction, pacing, cookies | Auctions decide each impression; pacing spreads budgets; IDs connect events.
adtech > experimentation | Campaign effectiveness is measured with lift tests.
identity > idgraph, cookies, privacytech | Profiles link identifiers, under privacy constraints.
experimentation > sampling | Each arm is a random sample of users.
experimentation > hypothesis, clt | Differences are judged with statistical tests that rest on the central limit theorem.
dimmodel > relmodel, normalization, joins | A star schema is a relational design that denormalizes dimensions for fast joins.
warehouse > dimmodel | Most warehouses are organized as star schemas.
datamesh > schemaevol | Contracts mean versioned schemas with compatibility rules.
datamesh > catalog | Data products are published through a catalog.
multitenancy > nscgroups, ratelimit, partitioning | Isolation, quotas and per-tenant partitions.
durableexec > wal, idempotence, outbox | Recorded history, safe replays and compensations.
workflow > durableexec | Modern orchestrators add durable execution.
cicd > swe, dst | Automated testing, from unit tests to simulation.
vcs > cicd | Commits drive pipelines.

## New techniques downward
logcompaction > hashtables, filesystems | The cleaner builds a map of latest offsets per key and rewrites segments.
logcompaction > lsm | The same idea as LSM compaction: keep the newest version per key.
consumergroups > partitioning, leaderelect | A coordinator assigns partitions to members.
consumergroups > dstheory | Rebalancing must handle members that are slow or dead.
tieredstorage > sepstorage, memhier | Hot data on local disk, cold data in object storage.
zorder > zonemaps, sorting | Sorting by interleaved keys tightens each file's min/max range.
zorder > binary | Z-order interleaves the bits of several columns.
compaction > filesystems | Fewer, bigger files mean less metadata and fewer opens.
compaction > bigo | Listing and opening N files costs O(N) round-trips.
schemaevol > typesys, parsing | Compatibility is a type-checking question about old and new schemas.
volcano > relalgebra, branchpred | Algebra operators connected by next(); per-row calls hurt CPU pipelines.
retries > probability, queueing | Random jitter spreads retries; retry storms overload queues.
circuitbreaker > control | A breaker is a feedback controller on error rate.
circuitbreaker > failuredetect | It trips on observed failures and timeouts.
fencing > leaderelect, hwclocks | Leases expire by clock, so an old holder may still act; fencing blocks it.
antientropy > merkle, hashfn | Replicas compare Merkle trees to find differing ranges.
outbox > acid, wal | The event and the change commit in one transaction; CDC can publish it.
pbft > dstheory, combinatorics | With 3f+1 nodes, any two quorums of 2f+1 share an honest node.
pow > hashfn, probability, gametheory | Finding a small hash is a lottery; honest mining is the profitable strategy.
dst > dstheory, montecarlo | Random fault schedules explore rare interleavings, replayable by seed.
shardpercore > scheduling, cachelines, numa | No lock contention or cache bouncing between cores.
autoscaling > control, queueing | Scale on queue length or utilization with a feedback loop.
oauth > signatures, hashfn | A JWT is signed claims; servers verify the signature.
mtls > tls, signatures | Both sides present certificates during the TLS handshake.
privacytech > probability, statistics | Differential privacy adds calibrated random noise.
scalinglaws > powerlaws, statistics, optimization | Empirical power-law fits that guide compute budgets.
cot > probability, learningtheory | Intermediate tokens give the model more computation per answer.
decoding > probability, infotheory | Sampling from a temperature-scaled softmax.
contrastive > metricspaces, infotheory | A loss that ranks the true pair above negatives (InfoNCE).
specdecode > probability, kvcache | Rejection sampling keeps the big model's exact output distribution.
promptcache > kvcache, hashtables, trees | Prefixes are looked up in a hash table or radix tree of KV blocks.
evals > statistics, hypothesis | Score differences need confidence intervals.
crossval > statistics, learningtheory | Held-out error estimates generalization error.
dct > fourier, linalg | The DCT is a Fourier-type change of basis.
dct > perception | Coarser quantization of high frequencies that the eye barely notices.
motioncomp > optimization | The encoder searches for the best matching block.
abr > control, queueing | The player is a controller keeping the buffer from running dry.
fingerprint > fourier, hashtables | Spectrogram peaks hashed and looked up in an index.
perceptualhash > dct, metricspaces | Low-frequency DCT bits compared by Hamming distance.
auction > gametheory | Bidding strategies are a game; second-price auctions reward truthful bids.
pacing > control | A feedback loop adjusts bids to hit the spend target.
idgraph > graphsalgo, probability | Connected components over links, weighted by confidence.
cookies > http | Set-Cookie and Cookie headers.

## New foundations
lsm, btree > externalmem | Both are designed to minimize block transfers.
linux > ebpf, allocators | The kernel runs eBPF and manages memory allocation.
langruntime > interpreters, allocators, unicode | Runtimes interpret bytecode, allocate memory and handle text.
floatingpoint > binary | Floats are a binary encoding of real numbers.
webarch > unicode | The web is UTF-8.
binary > boolalg, numbertheory | Addition with carries is Boolean logic; overflow is arithmetic modulo 2^n.
binary > leibniz | Leibniz wrote up binary arithmetic in 1703.
unicode > binary, codingtheory | A variable-length code with self-synchronizing byte patterns.
allocators > trees, queuesbuf | Free lists, buddy trees and size-class bins.
ebpf > jit, graphsalgo | The verifier walks the control-flow graph; programs are JIT-compiled.
interpreters > parsing, turingmachine | Parse to bytecode, then a dispatch loop runs it: a universal machine.
externalmem > asymptotics | Asymptotic cost counted in block transfers.
parallelism, batchproc, hpc, gpucomp > amdahl | The serial part limits speed-up.
amdahl > asymptotics | Speed-up approaches 1/serial fraction.
eviction, partitioning, cachelayer > powerlaws | Real access patterns are skewed; a few keys get most traffic.
powerlaws > probability | Heavy-tailed distributions.
sampling, hll > clt | Estimates concentrate around the truth as samples grow.
statistics > clt | Confidence intervals come from the CLT.
clt > probability | A theorem of probability.
hypothesis > probability, clt | Test statistics are approximately normal.
hypothesis > epistemology | How much evidence justifies a belief.
montecarlo > probability, clt | Error shrinks like 1/√n.
encoding, learningtheory > kolmogorov | Compression and simplicity are measured by description length.
kolmogorov > infotheory | Expected Kolmogorov complexity matches entropy.
kolmogorov > churchturing, occam | Defined with Turing machines; a formal Occam's razor.
cpucomp, gpucomp > mooreslaw | Decades of cheaper transistors paid for every speed-up.
mooreslaw > transistor, lithography, thermo | Smaller transistors from better lithography, until heat stopped frequency scaling.
perception > neurons | Perception is computed by neural circuits.

## New sideways
mesos = kubernetes : evol | Kubernetes largely replaced Mesos for cluster scheduling.
yarn = kubernetes : alt | Hadoop-era scheduler versus container orchestration.
hive = spark : evol | Spark SQL replaced Hive's MapReduce engine for most workloads.
memcached = redis : alt | Plain cache versus rich data structures with persistence.
scylla = cassandra : alt | Same data model and API, different engine.
tidb = cockroach : alt | Distributed SQL; TiDB adds a columnar replica for HTAP.
istio = cilium : alt | Sidecar mesh versus kernel-level networking.
haproxy = nginx : alt | Two event-driven proxies.
temporal = airflow : alt | Durable workflow code versus scheduled DAGs.
dbt = airflow : comp | dbt builds SQL models; Airflow schedules them.
mlflow = feast : comp | Model tracking and feature serving.
llamacpp = vllm : alt | Local single-user inference versus datacenter serving.
sglang = vllm : alt | Competing high-throughput serving engines.
a2a = mcp : comp | Agent-to-agent versus agent-to-tool.
cursor = codingagents : alt | Editor-first versus terminal-first coding agents.
amdgpu = nvgpu : alt | AMD versus NVIDIA accelerators.
riscv = armisa : alt | Open versus licensed RISC instruction sets.
avro = parquet : comp | Row-oriented messages versus columnar files.
volcano = vectorized : evol | Vectorized and compiled execution replaced row-at-a-time iterators.
pbft = raft : alt | Byzantine faults versus crash faults.
pow = pbft : alt | Open membership by cost versus fixed membership by voting.
retries = circuitbreaker : comp | Retry briefly, stop when a dependency is clearly down.
dimmodel = normalization : alt | Star schemas deliberately denormalize for analytics.
specdecode = contbatch : comp | Two ways to keep GPUs busy.
